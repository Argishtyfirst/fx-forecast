# FX Forecast Tool: full review and feature suggestions

*Read-only review of the code and the live site on 21 Oct 2026. Nothing was changed.*

## 1. What the app does today

**Data pipeline**
- Daily exchange rates for EUR/USD, USD/AMD and EUR/AMD (plus RUB/AMD) from Frankfurter. Late corrections are picked up, and the job fails if today's rate is still missing after 17:00 CET.
- Macro data from FRED (Fed rate, US 2-year yield, Brent oil, broad dollar index) and from the ECB (deposit rate, €STR, inflation). Each series has a "when was this actually published" rule, so backtests do not cheat.
- Every hour it checks Fed, ECB and CBA decisions: the new rates, the statement text, what changed since the last statement, and a hawkish/dovish tone score from Gemini.
- Meeting calendars for all three banks. The CBA schedule PDF draws its dates as images, and the app decodes them.
- Fed surprises are measured against market pricing from the Atlanta Fed probability tracker.
- GitHub Actions runs it all: daily, hourly and weekly jobs. Every job run is logged, and one failing source does not stop the others.

**Forecasting**
- Uncertainty ranges (50/80/95%) for 1 day, 1 week and 1 month. They come from a GARCH model, get wider around central bank meetings, and are calibrated on past errors.
- LightGBM and interest-rate-parity forecasts compete with a "no change" baseline. A model is only used if it proves better than that baseline.
- Backtest from 2010 onward, with separate scores for weeks that contain meetings.
- Meeting reaction study: how each pair moved before, on and after Fed, ECB and CBA meetings.

**AI layer**
- A daily Gemini view per pair and horizon: chance the rate goes up, expected change, confidence and short reasoning. It is scored live against what actually happens.
- A weekly Gemini note with "What it means for you" and "Watch next".

**Website** (React, hosted on Vercel, Neon Postgres database)
- A "Sell EUR for AMD now?" card with a horizon picker phrased as a need, a verdict, a confidence meter, the AI track record and meeting warnings.
- Fan chart with 90 days of history, forecast ranges and meeting markers.
- Four tabs: Pair, Drivers (why the forecast looks like this), Events (meeting reaction charts and past decisions), Backtest (accuracy against targets).
- Data freshness line with a warning when a source is out of date.
- Light and dark theme, works on phones, deep links to each view.

## 2. What is weak or missing

Highest impact first.

1. **The big "Sell now" comes from the unproven AI.** For EUR/AMD at 1W and 1M the card shows "Sell now, clear", while the statistical answer is "no edge". All 9 pair-horizons show sell_now, and 0 of 250 AI calls have been checked. The same card says the AI "does not decide the verdict".
2. **Nothing is shown in money.** You can't type how many euros you have and see the AMD you'd get now or later. The data for it (carry, expected change) is already sent to the page and never used.
3. **Only the CBA average rate is used.** The rate a bank actually pays you, and which bank pays best, are not shown. That gap is often worth more than any timing decision.
4. **No alerts for you.** Nothing tells you when EUR/AMD reaches your target, when the verdict changes, or that a Fed meeting is tomorrow. You have to open the site to learn anything.
5. **No history of past calls.** You can't see what the app said each day next to what the rate then did. The verdict is recalculated on every page load and never saved.
6. **The AI call changes during the day without new data.** Each extra cron run overwrites the earlier answer. Today the 1D EUR/AMD call went from sell_now to wait in 7 minutes, so two visits in one evening can show opposite advice.
7. **A bad rate is stored in the data.** EUR/AMD on 26 May 2026 is 367.74, which is that day's USD/AMD rate (the real value is about 428). It distorts the 1-year low, the volatility model and the backtests, and no check would have caught it.
8. **FRED is failing in production right now.** No API key is set, and the free CSV route returns an HTML page. The site shows "degraded", the Fed rate and US yields go stale, and the AI loses that part of its input.
9. **The AI can't earn trust in any reasonable time.** Promotion needs 250 live calls (about a year), and the count restarts on every model or prompt change. It already restarted today. The test also scores it on the wrong thing: error size, not whether it got the direction right.
10. **The app opens on EUR/USD**, not EUR/AMD, and only remembers the horizon, not the pair.
11. **No "is today's rate good or bad?" context.** Only 90 days of history, no 1-year or 5-year range, and no daily change next to the spot rate.
12. **The most useful plain-English text is buried.** The weekly "What it means for you" note sits at the bottom of the technical Backtest tab.
13. **Frankfurter is the only rate source.** If it goes down, the whole product goes blank. The backup the spec describes was never built.
14. **Nobody gets told when the pipeline breaks.** No Slack, email or Telegram channel is configured. The daily run also reports success when FRED or the AI fails, so GitHub's own failure email stays quiet.
15. **The health check hides real problems.** It ignores failed jobs, and a job killed by a timeout stays "running" forever. That happened today.
16. **Gemini quota is wasted.** In summer time the AI view can run up to 4 times a day, and the hourly tone scorer uses the same model and the same free quota.
17. **ECB and CBA "surprises" are measured against the previous rate**, so a fully expected cut counts as a full surprise. This skews the surprise filter and what the AI is told about meetings.
18. **Meeting-week warnings rest on very little data**, about 8 recent meetings per bank. The roughly 130 meetings per bank since 2010 are never scored.
19. **No model beats "no change"**, so the statistical answer is always "no edge". For currencies at these horizons this is the expected result, not a bug, but it is why the hero leans on the AI.
20. **Little Armenia-side data.** There is no Armenian inflation, CBA interventions, reserves or remittances. The AI also doesn't get the US yields, oil, dollar index and Fed market odds that are already ingested.
21. **Small UI defects.** The AI row shows as raw "llm" and sorts first. The chart readout disappears when you lift your finger on a phone. "Watch next" date chips never render. EUR/AMD Drivers shows "Fed policy rate n/a". Raw parser errors and a "port 8000" message reach users.
22. **The Events tab lists 46 upcoming meetings out to 2028** and ignores which pair you are looking at.
23. **No database backups.** The API connects as the database owner, and the year-long AI track record lives only on Neon's free tier.

## 3. Suggested features, ranked

Ranked by how useful each one is to you day to day, then by value, then by effort (S = hours to a day, M = a few days, L = a week or more). None of these need paid data. Where an idea depends on scraping or on a free key, the Notes column says so.

| Rank | Feature | What it does for you | Effort | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Honest, stable headline** | The big verdict becomes the statistical answer ("no proven edge, waiting only adds risk"). The AI lean moves to a smaller "AI hunch, unproven (n of 250)" line. The AI is called once per day, a new call only replaces it when the input data changed, and the card shows "yesterday vs today". | S | Fixes the most misleading thing on the page. Also cuts Gemini calls from up to 4 a day to about 1. Add a dead zone so 0.49 is not shown as "sell". |
| 2 | **"My euros in AMD" calculator, opening on EUR/AMD** | You type €5,000 and see 2,029,600 AMD today, 1,984,650 to 2,075,600 AMD likely in a month, and about +7,200 AMD earned by converting now (carry). The app opens on EUR/AMD and remembers your amount and pair. | S | Uses data the API already sends. The amount stays in your browser. Label it "at the CBA rate" until rank 3 exists. Optional fields for your own deposit rates. |
| 3 | **Best bank to convert today** | Hourly buy/sell rates from Armenian banks (via rate.am or bank sites). Shows the top 3 banks for selling EUR, the net AMD for your amount, each bank's gap to the CBA rate, and EUR to USD to AMD vs direct. | M | The biggest real-money gain: a 2 to 3 AMD per euro gap between banks is worth more than a month of carry. Free but unofficial scraping, so it will break when sites change. Check the sites' terms and label quotes "indicative". |
| 4 | **Telegram alerts and daily digest** | Messages when EUR/AMD reaches your target, when the verdict changes (damped so it doesn't spam), the day before Fed, ECB or CBA meetings, plus one daily line, for example "EUR/AMD 405.92, near 1-year low, no edge, Fed in 7 days". The same bot also tells you when the pipeline breaks or recovers. | M | Free Telegram bot. Targets and chat id go in GitHub secrets, never in the public repo. Group alerts so there is at most one per problem per day. |
| 5 | **"Is today a good rate?" context, plus a data fix** | A daily change badge and 1Y/5Y range bars with a plain sentence. Today it would say a euro buys close to the fewest AMD of the past year (within 0.4% of the 12-month low). | S | Repair the bad 26 May row first and add a cross-rate check (EUR/AMD vs EUR/USD x USD/AMD). Otherwise the 1-year low is wrong. Label it "context, not a forecast". |
| 6 | **"This week for your euros" card** | One card on the main page: countdown to the next Fed (28 Oct), ECB (29 Oct) and CBA (3 Nov) meetings, the meeting-risk sentences, and the weekly "What it means for you" note moved up from the Backtest tab. | S | Also fixes the broken "Watch next" chips. Show the date of the weekly note. |
| 7 | **Past calls log, in AMD** | Each day's verdict and AI call is saved. Once the target date passes, it shows hit or miss and the AMD gained or lost per €1,000 by following it, next to "always convert now" and a coin flip. | M | The fastest honest way to judge the AI, long before the 250-call promotion. Show "too few to judge" early on, because 1-month calls overlap. |
| 8 | **Pipeline quick fixes** | Set the free FRED API key with automatic fallback between the API and CSV routes, roll back the AI job per pair so one database error can't lose the whole run, and mark stuck "running" jobs as failed. | S | Not a visible feature, but FRED is broken today, and these keep the other features working. Most of it takes minutes. |
| 9 | **Freshness stamp on the verdict** | Always shows "Call for Wed 21 Oct, made 23:58 Yerevan, next update Thu". It turns amber when the call is old. Health counts failed forecast and AI jobs, and raw errors become plain text like "US rate data delayed, today's ranges not affected". | S | Use the pipeline's own working-day calendar so holidays don't raise false alarms. Show Yerevan time, since that is where you are. |
| 10 | **Target chance: "will EUR/AMD reach 410?"** | Type a rate and see the chance it ends above it, and the chance it touches it at any point, for 1D/1W/1M. One tap turns it into a Telegram alert. | M | The "ends above" chance is easy from the existing ranges. The "touches" chance needs a small simulation in the daily job. It is not a forecast edge: it is still based on the "no change" model. |
| 11 | **Split-it planner** | Compares all now, thirds (now, after Fed, after CBA) and "now unless it reaches Y". Shows the likely worst and best AMD for each plan, plus a replay of each plan over every month since 2010. | M | The honest answer when no model has an edge: splitting cuts the worst case, not the average. Shares the simulation with rank 10. |
| 12 | **"How to read this" explainer and glossary** | 4 short cards on first visit and tap-to-open definitions (80% range, carry, hit rate, coverage), filled with live numbers, for example "8 times in 10 the rate landed inside the 1M range". | S | Helps if you show it to family. Render the numbers from the API so the text doesn't go out of date. |
| 13 | **Installable phone app (PWA)** | Home-screen icon that opens on EUR/AMD and still shows the last call (with its date) when offline or when the API is down. | M | Free. Needed later for web push on iPhone. Add a "new version, reload" prompt so you don't get stuck on an old version. |
| 14 | **Backup rate source and cross-check** | If Frankfurter fails, rates come straight from the ECB and CBA. On days both work, they are compared, and sudden jumps are flagged before the forecast runs. | M | Removes the single point of failure. The CBA service is old and may change its format. |
| 15 | **Richer AI input** | Gives Gemini the already-ingested US 2-year yield, oil, dollar index and Fed market odds. Later adds a calendar of US/EU data releases (CPI, jobs, euro inflation). | S to M | The first part is S. The release calendar is M and needs a free source picked. |
| 16 | **Early read on the AI without resetting its record** | Re-runs the current AI on past days after its training cutoff, scored separately and never counted for promotion. A new prompt or model runs in parallel with the old one, so the count doesn't restart. | M | About 450 one-off Gemini calls. There is a real risk the model already "knows" what happened, so it needs a leak check, and its results must stay apart from the live record. |
| 17 | **Trustworthy meeting warnings** | Scores meeting weeks over all ~130 meetings per bank since 2010 instead of about 8, uses separate range calibration for meeting weeks, and puts error bars on coverage numbers. | M | Tells you whether "bigger swings in Fed week" is real or noise. |
| 18 | **Weekly encrypted backup of the track record** | A weekly encrypted copy of the database in a private R2 or B2 bucket, a restore command, and a read-only database user for the website. | S | Cheap insurance: the AI's live record can't be rebuilt if it is lost. |

**Parked on purpose:**
- Better ECB/CBA surprise measures: useful, but M effort and mostly a modelling-quality gain.
- Armenia managed-float signal (CBA interventions, reserves, remittances): L effort, monthly data arrives 4 to 6 weeks late, and the cba.am parsing is fragile.
- Options-implied volatility from FRED EVZ: needs the FRED key, and the series may be discontinued.
- Intraday EUR/USD around meetings: L effort, data-terms questions, and outside the spec's scope.
- Several-model AI ensemble: only worth doing after rank 7 exists to judge it.
- Armenian/Russian language and a share button: nice, but low priority for one user.

### Top 3 to do next

1. **Honest, stable headline (S):** today the biggest text on the page tells you to sell euros based on a model with no checked calls, and that call can flip within minutes, so fixing this comes first.
2. **"My euros in AMD" calculator, opening on EUR/AMD (S):** it turns the page into the decision you actually make, "how many AMD for my euros, and how much could waiting move that", using data already being sent.
3. **Best bank to convert today (M):** choosing the bank is the one gain you can measure today, and it is usually bigger than anything timing can promise.

Separately, setting the free FRED API key takes about five minutes and fixes today's "degraded" status. It is worth doing alongside any of these.
